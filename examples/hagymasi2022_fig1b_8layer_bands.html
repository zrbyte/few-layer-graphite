Result

The model reproduces the structure of the panel. Two surface bands stay within a few meV of each other out to about 0.03 Å⁻¹ from K and then separate into an electron and a hole branch. On both sides a ladder of bulk subbands follows, closer to the Fermi level towards Γ than towards M. The flat band lands where the paper has it relative to EF.

The bulk subbands lie 11 to 23 % further from EF than in the ab initio calculation. The ±0.32 eV window therefore holds two of them above and two below EF in the model (the second conduction subband only on the Γ side), where the panel holds three.

Where the model departs from the panel

  • Subband energies. Conduction subbands are 17 to 23 % and valence subbands 11 to 17 % further from EF in the model. At K the model's dimer bands meet near −0.37 and +0.40 eV, outside the window. In the panel they meet at the frame edges, near ±0.32 eV. In the model these energies are Δ′ ± γ1 (up to the small γ2 terms, and minus EF), which points to a smaller interlayer coupling in the ab initio bands than γ1 = 0.39 eV.
  • Left–right asymmetry. In the panel the subband extrema sit at nearly the same distance from K on both sides. In the model the Γ-side extrema lie 16 to 28 % further out. The energy difference between the two sides, in contrast, agrees with the panel in sign and to within 4 meV in size (first conduction subband: 7 meV in the panel, 11 meV in the model; first valence subband: 17 and 16 meV). Both asymmetries are set by the skew coupling γ3 in the model: with γ3 = 0 both reverse.
  • Flat-band depth. 12 meV in the model against 18 meV in the panel, a difference of 5 meV against a reading error of about ±2 meV.
  • k scale. The panel has no k axis. The half-width of 0.137 Å⁻¹ comes from matching the outermost branches of model and panel (least squares; 0.145 Å⁻¹ from the Γ side alone, 0.128 Å⁻¹ from the M side alone). This choice absorbs any difference in band velocity between the two calculations: if the ab initio velocity is lower than the model's 1.0 × 10⁶ m/s, the true window of the panel is wider in proportion. The energies in the table do not depend on it.

How it was calculated

Path and Fermi level

The bands are evaluated on the line Γ ← K → M, at 1601 points within the half-width of K. The energy zero is the Fermi level of the neutral slab. The model is a semimetal: the valence surface band rises above the conduction-band minimum at K. EF is the energy at which the electron pocket and the hole pocket around K hold the same number of states, counted on a 401 × 401 mesh within 0.08 Å⁻¹ of K. It lies 9.6 meV above the surface-state energy at K.

The published panel

Fig. 1B is embedded in the paper's PDF as a raster. It was rendered from page 2 at 600 dpi. The energy scale comes from the three tick marks (0.2, 0 and −0.2 eV), which put the frame at ±0.320 eV. K is at the centre of the frame. The supplement's Fig. S15 labels the same path Γ ← K → M and the energy axis E − EF.

The paper's ab initio calculations use SIESTA with the PBE functional and a double-ζ polarised basis (supplement, section S2.1). The panel shows no interaction-induced splitting of the surface state, like the bands labelled “GGA, no interaction” in Fig. S15a.
